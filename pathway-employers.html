        Accountancy Curriculum Evidence
        The baccalaureate consists of 54.5 hours and combines accounting depth with a shared business core and applied experience.

        Prerequisites

        Accounting, economics, and quantitative foundations.

        ACC 111BEM 201ECN 150MTH 111 or 112

        Business core

        Organizations, markets, operations, data, and strategy.

        ACC 221BEM 211BEM 221BEM 241BEM 251BEM 261BEM 371/372BEM 388FIN 231

        Required major

        Reporting, audit, tax, systems, and professional judgment.

        ACC 211ACC 212ACC 237ACC 351ACC 352ACC 390ACC 392/393FIN 232

        Recommended preparation

        • ACC 391 is strongly recommended.
        • ACC 311 and ACC 321 are strongly recommended for students not pursuing the MSA.
        • A ten-week paid accounting or finance internship is strongly recommended and built into the senior-year curriculum.
        • Accountancy students may apply to the MSA during junior year.
        Planning notice: Revised Accountancy requirements apply to students admitted Spring 2027 and continuing as juniors in 2027–28. Confirm your catalog year with official advising.
